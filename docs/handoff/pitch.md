# Pitch — Physio-Aid

## One-line pitch

Physio-Aid helps older adults prolong healthspan by combining falls efficacy profiling and chair stand movement analytics into an ability–confidence profile that guides practical care recommendations and community linkage.

## Opening hook

Frailty and falls are closely linked threats to healthy ageing. The brief highlights that in Singapore, approximately 6.2% of older adults are frail and 37% are pre-frail, while about one in three older adults are at risk of falls, with risk rising further among those aged 80 and above.

Falls are not just accidents. They can be sentinel events in the frailty life-course, accelerating functional decline, loss of confidence, reduced participation, hospitalisation, and care dependence.

## Problem

Current care is often reactive. Older adults may decline between visits. Caregivers or community workers may notice that someone is slower, less confident, or avoiding movement, but they may not have a structured way to interpret what is happening.

The gap is not only movement ability. It is the relationship between ability and confidence.

## Solution

Physio-Aid is a web-based physiotherapy support platform. It guides an older adult through:
- safety screening and consent
- emergency contact setup
- demographic information
- four falls efficacy questions
- a chair stand functional assessment
- smartphone accelerometer / motion sensor input
- computer vision or sensor-derived analytics
- rule-based ability–confidence profiling
- risk stratification
- dashboard, report, recommendations, and care linkage

## Innovation

Most digital health tools do one thing: track activity, show exercise videos, or collect questionnaires. Physio-Aid combines objective functional performance with perceived confidence.

The key innovation is the ability–confidence profile:

| Profile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Good ability + low confidence | Under-confidence, avoidance, fear of movement |
| Poor ability + high confidence | Possible risk-taking or reduced insight |
| Poor ability + low confidence | High vulnerability |
| Good ability + good confidence | Stable profile |

Pitch line:

> Our innovation is not just detecting movement. It is interpreting the gap between what an older adult can do and what they believe they can do, then translating that into personalised action.

## Technical implementation

The MVP uses:
- web app workflow
- browser camera capture
- smartphone accelerometer input
- chair stand assessment
- questionnaire scoring
- rule-based analytics
- dashboard and report generation

For the hackathon, the app demonstrates one complete pathway: Mr Tan completes screening, answers four confidence questions, performs a chair stand test, and receives an ability–confidence profile with recommendations and care linkage.

## Feasibility

Physio-Aid is community-ready because it can work with:
- a phone, tablet, or laptop camera
- a smartphone accelerometer
- a chair
- a short questionnaire
- a web-based dashboard

It does not require expensive laboratory infrastructure.

## Safety and accountability

Physio-Aid is decision support, not diagnosis. It includes consent, emergency contact setup, pre-assessment safety screening, privacy-aware derived metrics, and care linkage to Active Ageing Centres, Community Health Posts, or Physiotherapy Clinics.

## Closing

Physio-Aid helps Singapore move from reactive falls care to proactive healthspan extension: measuring movement, confidence, and care needs before a fall happens.
