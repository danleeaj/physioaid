# Ethics, Safety, Privacy, and Regulatory Positioning

## Ethical stance

Physio-Aid supports older adults by making movement and confidence information easier to interpret. It must not pressure unsafe movement, overclaim medical value, or replace professional judgement.

## Clinical accountability

Use:
- decision support
- screening
- care recommendations
- suggested follow-up
- community linkage

Avoid:
- diagnosis
- prescription
- clinically validated
- predicts falls
- certifies safe
- replaces physiotherapist

## Consent model

Separate:
1. Assessment consent
2. Optional research consent

Assessment consent should cover:
- purpose of assessment
- movement test safety
- data collected
- report generation
- decision-support limitation

Research consent should cover:
- anonymised derived data
- future Singapore mobility research
- optional participation
- no effect on assessment if declined

## Safety model

Before movement:
- safety screening
- emergency contact
- stable chair instructions
- clear space instructions
- supervision prompt if risk is present

Stop conditions:
- dizziness
- chest pain
- severe pain
- unusual breathlessness
- feeling unsafe
- recent fall or injury
- unstable chair or environment

If stop condition exists:
- do not continue normal assessment
- recommend support or clinical/community review
- allow demo mode only when clearly marked

## Privacy model

Prefer:
- derived movement metrics
- local processing where possible
- minimal personal data
- short retention
- anonymised research exports

Avoid:
- raw video storage by default
- unnecessary identifiers
- background tracking
- hidden analytics
- combining emergency contacts with research datasets

## PDPA-aware controls

For MVP documentation and UI:
- explain purpose
- collect only needed data
- request consent before collection
- limit access
- separate research use
- provide export/delete language if storage is implemented
- protect emergency contact data
- avoid unnecessary retention

## Bias and accessibility

Potential risks:
- older adults with mobility limitations may be unable to complete chair stand
- sensor/camera metrics may vary by device, lighting, clothing, chair height, and environment
- language and digital literacy may affect responses
- confidence may be culturally or personally expressed differently

Mitigations:
- allow "unable to complete" as valid data
- show manual fallback
- use plain language
- keep UI high contrast and large
- do not interpret inability as diagnosis
- encourage professional review when uncertain

## Recommendation ethics

Recommendations should be conservative and supportive:
- maintain safe activity
- build confidence gradually
- seek support if needed
- consider community or physiotherapy review

Do not:
- prescribe exercise dosage as medical treatment
- suggest high-risk unsupervised activity
- imply emergency response unless integrated and validated
- shame users for low confidence or poor ability

## Report disclaimer

Every report should include:
> Physio-Aid provides a decision-support summary based on self-reported confidence and simple movement assessment data. It is not a diagnosis and does not replace assessment by a qualified healthcare professional. If you feel unwell, unsafe, or have recently fallen or been injured, seek appropriate help before doing movement tests.
