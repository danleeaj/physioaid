# Business and Translation Model

## Product positioning

Physio-Aid is a web-based physiotherapy support platform for older-adult healthspan, frailty prevention, and falls-related decision support.

It is not positioned as:
- a consumer fitness app
- a generic fall detector
- a replacement for clinicians
- a hospital-only system
- a native mobile app-first product

## Value proposition

For older adults:
- understand movement and confidence
- receive practical next steps
- maintain independence safely

For caregivers:
- receive a shareable report
- understand when support or review may be helpful
- avoid guessing based on observation alone

For Active Ageing Centres and community care:
- structured screening workflow
- triage support
- clear care linkage

For physiotherapy clinics:
- between-session functional monitoring
- structured pre-visit summary
- confidence and function context

For researchers:
- optional anonymised benchmark data for Singapore mobility research, with explicit consent

## MVP business thesis

The MVP demonstrates that a simple web assessment can become a community-ready care pathway:
- accessible tools
- minimal training
- short assessment
- clinically meaningful output
- shareable report
- pathway to care

## Stakeholders

- older adults
- caregivers and family members
- care workers
- Active Ageing Centres
- Community Health Posts
- physiotherapy clinics
- rehabilitation services
- public health and ageing researchers

## Adoption path

### Hackathon

Show one polished journey and decision-support output.

### Pilot

Run supervised assessments in a community or clinic setting under Shaun's clinical guidance. Validate usability, safety, and workflow fit before making stronger claims.

### Community deployment

Support care workers with guided assessment and triage. Keep clinician review available for higher-risk profiles.

### Research extension

Collect anonymised derived metrics with optional consent to build local Singapore reference data.

## Revenue possibilities

These are future possibilities, not MVP requirements:
- B2B subscription for community care providers
- clinic dashboard or report management
- screening event toolkit
- anonymised research dataset partnership with proper governance
- enterprise deployment for eldercare networks

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Overclaiming medical value | Use decision-support language |
| Privacy concerns | Derived metrics, consent, minimal data |
| Safety during movement tests | Screening, emergency contact, supervision prompts |
| Poor older-adult usability | Large typography, minimal steps, high contrast |
| Integration delays | One patient-first path, demo fixtures, fallbacks |
| Clinical disagreement | Shaun final clinical review |

## Success metric for hackathon

The judges should believe:
- the problem is important
- the clinical logic is meaningful
- the tech is feasible
- the scope is realistic
- the path to deployment is credible
